# StateGuard SEO Strategy

> The strategic brain of the StateGuard-SEO-AI project. **Every agent reads this** to understand not just *what* to optimize, but *how StateGuard wins business*. When a recommendation could serve either traffic or leads, it serves **leads**.

**Last updated:** 2026-06-11 · Owner: SEO Director

---

## 1. Business Goals

### Primary goal — **Generate qualified security service leads**
The system exists to drive enquiries from organisations that can buy security services: site walkthroughs, quote requests, tender invitations, RFP shortlistings. Every keyword, page, brief, and GEO play is judged first on its ability to produce a **qualified lead**.

### Secondary goal — **Increase authority within the Australian security industry**
Be recognised — by buyers, by search engines, and by AI answer engines — as a credible, licensed, national integrated-security provider. Authority is the multiplier that makes the lead-gen engine convert; it is not pursued for its own sake.

> **The two goals are ranked, not equal.** When they conflict, lead generation wins.

| 1 | **Facility Managers** | Site safety, contractor reliability, compliance, cost control | Quote / site assessment |
| 2 | **Security Managers** | Risk reduction, response times, reporting, accreditation | Capability comparison → RFP |
| 3 | **Property Managers** | Asset protection, tenant safety, after-hours cover | Quote / concierge proposal |

Content and AI-visibility work should speak to these roles by name and decision criteria — not to a generic "customer".

## 5. Success Metrics

Success is measured by **business outcomes**, in priority order:

1. **Leads generated** — enquiries / quote requests / tender invitations attributable to organic + AI search.
2. **Service page rankings** — positions for the 5 priority service keywords.
3. **Industry page rankings** — positions for the 9 priority industry keywords.
4. **AI citations** — StateGuard mentioned/cited by ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, AI Overviews for buyer queries.
5. **Tender visibility** — appearing where procurement teams and government buyers research suppliers.

### What is NOT success
- **Traffic volume alone is not success.** High-traffic informational pages that don't move buyers toward an enquiry are low priority.
- Rankings for queries with no commercial/lead intent are deprioritised unless they build topical authority that supports a money page.

---

## 6. How StateGuard wins business (apply this in every recommendation)

1. **Lead intent first.** Prioritise commercial/transactional queries and the 5 service + 9 industry money pages over informational volume. Informational/AEO content earns its place only by supporting a money page (internal links, authority, citations).
2. **Speak to the buyer.** Frame pages and answers around the 6 audiences' decision criteria (compliance, response time, licensing, coverage, cost-of-risk).
3. **Lead with differentiators.** Mission Talk, Assist App, NFC patrol verification, Grade A1 monitoring, national 7-office footprint, full state licensing. These are the reasons to choose StateGuard — surface them for SEO, AEO and GEO.
4. **Win procurement & tenders.** Government/procurement queries and "security companies for [sector]" questions are high-value; optimise for credibility signals (licences, accreditations, case evidence) that survive a probity check.
5. **Earn AI citations on buyer questions.** GEO targets are "best/who provides [service/industry] Australia" questions — not generic AI tips. Make facts extractable and authoritative.
6. **Authority serves conversion.** Build topical depth per service/industry so the money pages rank and get cited — authority is the means, leads are the end.

---

## 7. Prioritisation rule (for the SEO Director & all agents)

When scoring any opportunity, weight:

```
Priority ≈ (Lead intent × Audience fit × Differentiator strength) ÷ Effort
            ↑ heaviest weight on lead intent
```

- **Critical/High:** money pages (priority services + industries), tender/procurement visibility, conversion blockers.
- **Medium:** expansion industries, audience solution pages, supporting AEO answers.
- **Low:** purely informational/awareness content with no near-term lead path.

> All future recommendations must **prioritise lead generation over traffic volume.** If a proposal can't be traced to a lead, a ranking that drives leads, or an AI citation that influences a buyer, it is not a priority.
