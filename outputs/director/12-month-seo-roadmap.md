## Guiding principles (applied to every quarter)

1. **Leads over traffic** — money pages (services + industries) and conversion come before informational volume.
2. **Buyers first** — built for Facility Managers, Security Managers, Property Managers, Procurement Teams, Construction Companies, Government Buyers.
3. **Australian intent** — AU English, AU locations, AU licensing/ASIAL/AS-NZS framing.
4. **GEO + AI citation** — win "best/who provides … Australia" answers, not generic AI tips.
5. **Commercial keywords first** — Commercial/Transactional intent prioritised; informational earns its place only by supporting a money page.

**Sequencing logic:** Q1 builds the highest-value money pages + conversion + GEO baseline → Q2 completes core services and top industries → Q3 expands into new verticals + local + tenders → Q4 converts, links, and builds authority. Foundations (licences, schema, interlinking) already exist, so effort goes into pages, answers, and conversion — not re-plumbing.
